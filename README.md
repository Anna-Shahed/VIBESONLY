# VIBESONLY

**Scan your vibe. Find your soundtrack.**

VIBESONLY is a visual music discovery experience that connects the world around you with music. Upload or explore playlists, scan a room or object using your camera, extract its colours and visual mood, and discover songs whose artwork and musical identity match the captured vibe.

Built around the idea that **every space has a soundtrack**, VIBESONLY turns visual environments into music discovery.

### Core Experience

**Camera → Colours → Vibe → Music**

* Scan your surroundings with a phone camera or webcam
* Extract dominant colours and visual characteristics
* Generate a visual vibe profile
* Discover music that matches the vibe
* Browse album artwork through a visual music feed
* Save songs and build playlists
* Designed for mobile and desktop

VIBESONLY is designed as a visual-first music product rather than a conventional music dashboard — combining **computer vision, colour analysis, music discovery, and editorial-inspired interaction design**.
